# WAF In-Class Discussion: ShinyHunters and Oracle PeopleSoft

## Preparation

Before class, do **not** read the news article yet. We will watch the news report together first.

### 1. News Video

We will first watch the FOX News report:

[FOX News — ShinyHunters and Oracle PeopleSoft attacks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-63DF9Eogs)

### 2. News Article

After watching the video, read the following article:

[ShinyHunters Uses WAF Bypass Trick in Oracle PeopleSoft Attacks — BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/shinyhunters-uses-waf-bypass-trick-in-oracle-peoplesoft-attacks/)

## In-Class Activity

As you read the article, look for **terminology related to network security and defense**.

For each term you identify:

1. Write down the term.
2. Try to explain what it means in your own words.
3. Think about how it relates to protecting a system or network.
4. Be prepared to explain it to your classmates.

## Class Discussion

After everyone has had time to read the article, we will discuss the terminology as a class.

You may be asked to:

1. Name **one security-related term or computer network-related term** from the article.
2. Explain what the term means in their own words.

The goal is to connect the terminology in a real-world security incident to the concepts we have learned in class.
