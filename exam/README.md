# Midterm Exam Instructions

## 1. Contributing a Problem (Optional)

You may optionally contribute **one problem** to help shape the midterm exam.

- **One problem per student:** Submit one problem in any format you choose.
- **File format:** Submit your problem as a `.txt` or `.md` file.
- **Images:** If your problem includes an image, upload it separately as a `.png` or `.jpg` file.
- **Submission location:** Upload your files to **Submitty**.
- **Deadline:** Submit your problem by **Saturday (10/17/2026) at 4:00 p.m.**

Participation is optional. You may take the exam whether or not you submit a problem.

## 2. How Submitted Problems Will Be Used

I will create exam questions based on the problems students submit. The version that appears on the exam may differ substantially from the original submission, but I will aim to test the **same underlying knowledge and concepts**.

For example:

- If your submitted problem tests knowledge of **TCP protocol security**, the exam version will also test TCP protocol security.
- If your submitted problem tests knowledge of **ICMP redirect packets**, the exam version will also test ICMP redirect packets, though it may approach the topic from a different angle.

In other words, the wording, format, scenario, or specific task may change, but the exam question will aim to assess the same knowledge area as the submitted problem.

## 3. Compiled Collection of Submitted Problems

After the submission deadline, I will compile the submitted problems into a single PDF and post it on the **course calendar by 9:00 p.m. on Saturday (10/17/2026)**. Use this PDF to review the topics represented by the submitted problems.

## 4. Exam Content and Weighting

The exam will be divided into two parts:

- **60% — Modified versions of student-submitted problems**
- **40% — Questions on the following topics**

The remaining 40% of the exam will cover:

1. **TCP Wireshark traces:** Given a TCP trace, determine the TCP sequence numbers, acknowledgment numbers, and window size. The initial sequence number, acknowledgment number, and window size will be provided.
2. **Routing tables:** Given a routing table and a destination address, determine which interface will be used.
3. **IP addressing and network masks:** Given a network mask and two IP addresses, determine whether the addresses belong to the same network.
4. **DNS troubleshooting:** Diagnose and troubleshoot DNS-related problems.

## 5. Exam Materials and Prohibited Devices

- You may bring **crib sheets** to the exam, with **no limit on the number of pages**.
- **Electronic devices, including watches and smartwatches, are not permitted.**
